Satellite radar explains R²=0.528 of bunker sales variance alone (n=57) — weather adds nothing, and the signal survives detrending (YoY r=+0.46). The mechanism is confirmed by AIS: the eOPL is a tanker anchorage (70% of anchored reports), with median tanker dwell of 18 hours per visit. Negative results reported: OOS nowcast does not beat persistence; H1-2024 congestion was invisible in monthly counts; mega-ship consolidation was a detector artifact.
